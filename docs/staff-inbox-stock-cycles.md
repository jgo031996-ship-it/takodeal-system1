# Staff Inbox, refund deductions and cycle counts

New Owner-confirmed Grab/Foodpanda refund cases create one `Unpaid` Online Order Refund deduction for the confirmed actual refund, after the Owner records the case review and confirms the reviewed payroll policy and employee response opportunity. The amount is disclosed before confirmation. Unconfirmed complaints must have zero refund and create `No Charge` records. Original receipts, stock usage and platform settlements are preserved. Existing `Pending Review` cases require an explicit Apply deduction action; no bulk conversion occurs.

Staff receive a top deduction alert opening My Inbox. Refund notices include the receipt, amount, reason, evidence reference and a response/dispute field. Owner replies refresh the alert. Reading a notice records acknowledgement, never agreement or authorization. Private Inbox access and replies reuse the existing Owner-approved Staff document device binding. An unapproved phone can request access from the Inbox; the Owner approves it in Master Employee Profile. No PIN lookup, public profile field or arbitrary local staff ID grants private access.

Auto-generated Owner payslips show ONLINE REFUND ORDERS separately from Foods. Refunds use exact employee IDs; ambiguous or changed employee names require reconciliation. Paid payslips stay frozen. For payslips containing refund charges, payment account updates, the expense, saved payslip, loan/meal/advance settlement and refund case/notice settlement occur in one transaction. Repeated submissions and lost acknowledgements cannot create a second payment. Charged cases may be withdrawn by the Owner before payment; paid cases cannot be silently modified. Recorded employee profiles are reduced to the fields required for payroll, excluding login credentials and unrelated private records.

Cashier Stock Counts & History uses one compact list per restock cycle with search and cycle filtering. Blank entries remain unreported; explicit zero means no physical stock. Purchase quantities and loose base quantities are stored in a validated count snapshot. One submission saves inventory balances, stock logs, an Owner `stock_counts` audit with the fresh expected balance and cost snapshot, and a cycle-count dispatch report when stock is below the saved maintaining target. No printed-sheet quantities are hardcoded. The legacy count handler remains in source for retained regression coverage; the effective handler is installed by `stock-count-ui.js` after app setup.

Owner review and the dispatch cart show branch physical counts in their retained purchase units, the cycle maintaining target, stock needed to reach target, and Main Office available stock. Count colors are red for zero, orange below target and green when the target is met. Missing targets/conversion evidence are disclosed rather than guessed. An older saved dispatch suggestion stays visible when the original target is missing. Negative HQ availability displays as zero while the original balance remains traceable and the existing automatic restock/financial estimate workflow remains unchanged. Adding a count report to the cart starts sending quantity at zero for Owner review.

Both online Sales History cards are native buttons with delegated event handling. New modules are included in the offline app shells; receipt queues and photo stores are preserved.

Validation: run all `tests/*.test.mjs` except the separate browser outbox suite, parse app scripts, and run `tests/cancelled-food-rules-emulator.mjs` against its isolated demo emulator. `tests/build-inbox-stock-qa.mjs` creates phone samples using actual modules and actual Owner card markup, synthetic in-memory records, and CSP `connect-src 'none'`. Sample testing never creates production charges, payroll, inventory counts or device approvals. It does not establish legal compliance of a particular charge or prove behavior on every physical old device.
